## What is automatic

- **Skeletons share draw calls.** The runtime batches skeletons of up to 1024 vertices: 200 instances of a small rig draw in one call. A Pixi object between two skeletons in the draw order ends a batch. So keep skeletons next to each other in the display list.
- **A skeleton at rest costs close to nothing.** When its animation ended, or it never played one, no timeline runs and the runtime uploads nothing. It wakes with the next `play`.
- **Less overdraw, once you pass outlines.** Outlines are off by default. Write the outlines of an atlas at build time with `pixi-marrow outlines`. Then pass the file as `outlines`. The runtime then skips a region without a visible pixel, and a slot behind the opaque part of a later slot. The picture stays the same.

## Render mode

<Snippet source={source} regions="render-mode" />

`renderMode` is `auto` by default. The runtime then batches a skeleton of up to 1024 vertices, and gives a larger one meshes of its own. `mesh` and `batched` force one mode. `stats.renderMode` names the mode in use.

## Physics

<Snippet source={source} regions="physics" />

`physics` is the official mode: `update`, `pose`, `reset` or `none`. Physics does not follow the Pixi position by itself. Tell it with `physicsTranslate` and `physicsRotate`. A skeleton with physics constraints keeps simulating at rest.

## Versions

| What | Supported |
|---|---|
| Spine | Exports of 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3, as `.json` or `.skel` |
| PixiJS | Version 8, from 8.21 |
| Renderer | WebGL2 and WebGPU |

The runtime solves every constraint: IK, transform, path, physics and sliders. Clipping works.

## Limits

- No Canvas 2D and no WebGL1.
- No Spine 3.8 and no Spine 4.0.
- The runtime plays skeletons. To edit them, use the Spine editor.
- The numbers for speed come from one desktop machine. The only phone measured so far is a Galaxy A05, for the idle cache. See [Performance](/performance/).
- The licence is the Spine Runtimes License, as for the official runtimes. Each user of a product built on it needs a Spine Editor licence of their own.
