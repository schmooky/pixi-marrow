## The states of a symbol

Animators of slot symbols make a small set of animations, one per state. The names differ between studios, so read them from the files. These are the states of a Bzzz symbol:

| Animation | When | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `idle` | On the reels, at rest. It loops. | 1.33 s |
| `blur_in` | The reel starts to spin. The symbol turns blurred and stays so. | 0.27 s |
| `blur_out` | The reel stops. The symbol turns sharp again. | 0.27 s |
| `landing` | Right after the stop: a short bounce into the cell. | 0.33 s |
| `win` | The symbol is on a winning line. | 1.33 s |
| `disable` | The symbol is not on the winning line. A dimmed pose. | 0 s |

Special symbols have more. The coin of Bzzz also has `collect`, `ready`, `second_chance` and bonus versions of several states.

<Snippet source={source} regions="names" />

`info` lists the animations, skins, bones, slots and events of a skeleton, with the length of each animation. For names checked by TypeScript, `pixi-marrow typegen` writes them as types at build time.

## Rest

<Snippet source={source} regions="idle" />

`play` starts an animation on track 0 now, in place of what the track played. A symbol at rest loops its `idle`.

## Land, then rest

<Snippet source={source} regions="land" />

`queue` plays an animation after what the track holds now, with no gap between them. A landing is short and does not loop, so the symbol bounces once and then rests.

`mix: 0` starts the landing at once, without a blend from the blurred state. A landing that starts late looks as if the reel had stopped without the symbol.

## Mix between states

<Snippet source={source} regions="mix" />

A mix is the time one animation takes to blend into the next. `defaultMix` holds for every pair, and `setMix` for one pair. A short mix hides the cut between two states that end and start in different poses, such as the end of a win and the start of the idle loop.

## Hold a pose

<Snippet source={source} regions="hold" />

An animation that does not loop holds its last frame until the track plays something else. `disable` lasts 0 seconds, so it is a pose: the symbol stays dimmed until the next `play`. `blur_in` works the same way, and keeps the symbol blurred for as long as its reel spins.

## Know when a state ends

<Snippet source={source} regions="finished" />

`play` and `queue` return a `Track`, the handle on that animation. `finished` resolves when an animation that does not loop has played to its end. It also resolves when another `play` replaces the animation first, so a game that waits on a win must check whether the win was cut short.
